FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is Pool Up?

Pool Up is a work-route matching platform that connects verified commuters, drivers and approved transport partners travelling in the same direction.

Is Pool Up a Taxi Service?

Pool Up is designed around regular shared work routes rather than instant on-demand trips.

Certain routes may be provided by approved transport operators where required.

Who Can Use Pool Up?

The first version is intended for employed commuters, job seekers travelling to approved opportunities, verified drivers, employers and community partners.

Does Pool Up Operate Across Cape Town?

Routes are activated one corridor at a time.

Registering your route helps us decide where to launch next.

Does Registration Guarantee a Ride?

No.

A ride is only confirmed once there is a suitable verified driver or approved transport partner.

How Are Drivers Verified?

Drivers complete identity, licence, vehicle and relevant compliance checks before they can receive confirmed bookings.

Does Pool Up Own the Vehicles?

Not necessarily.

Pool Up works with verified private drivers and approved transport partners.

How Do Payments Work?

Passengers add prepaid ride credit.

Credit is used for confirmed journeys according to the pricing, cancellation and refund terms.

Can I Pay the Driver in Cash?

Passengers should not pay drivers directly unless Pool Up has provided written approval for a specific arrangement.

How Does Pool Up Make Money?

Pool Up earns a transparent service fee from completed bookings.

This supports verification, route coordination, payment administration, customer support and platform operations.

Can I Book a Return Journey?

The initial pilot prioritises journeys to the workplace.

Return routes will be added where demand and driver availability make them practical.

Can People With Disabilities Register?

Yes.

Accessibility requirements must be provided during registration.

A booking is confirmed only after a suitable vehicle and support arrangement have been identified.

What Happens When a Passenger Cancels?

The cancellation and refund policy depends on the time of cancellation, booking type and any costs already incurred.

How Can My Employer Join?

Employers can register staff travel demand, sponsor ride credit or request a shift-route pilot.

How Can a Foundation or Charity Participate?

Community organisations can sponsor ride credit, refer approved beneficiaries or work with Pool Up to create employment-access routes.

How Do I Report a Problem?

Use the safety reporting form, WhatsApp support or the official Pool Up contact details.

Immediate emergencies should be reported to emergency services first.
