Drive With Us

Your Route. Your Available Seats. More Value From Every Journey.

Many people drive to work with open seats while commuters nearby are searching for affordable transport.

Pool Up helps verified drivers connect with suitable people travelling in the same direction.

Apply as a Driver

Who Can Apply?

Pool Up is intended for responsible drivers who already travel along regular work routes.

Applicants must be willing to complete verification, follow agreed arrangements and treat every passenger with respect.

Driver Verification

Verification may include:

  • Valid identity documents
  • Valid driving licence
  • Vehicle ownership or authorised-use documents
  • Vehicle licence and roadworthiness information
  • Insurance information
  • Proof of address
  • References where required
  • Any licence, permit or compliance requirement applicable to the service
  • Vehicle condition checks
  • Driver conduct agreement

Approval is not automatic.

Pool Up may decline or suspend an application where information cannot be verified or safety concerns are identified.

How Driver Payments Work

Drivers see the proposed journey contribution before accepting a booking.

After a completed journey, the driver amount is recorded according to the agreed payout schedule.

Pool Up deducts a clearly disclosed service fee.

Drivers must not request cash from passengers, add unauthorised charges or change the agreed price.

The Pool Up Driver Promise

As a Pool Up driver, I agree to:

  • Drive responsibly
  • Follow the confirmed route and meeting arrangement
  • Keep my vehicle clean and roadworthy
  • Treat every passenger with dignity
  • Avoid harassment and discrimination
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Never request additional cash
  • Report delays, incidents and safety concerns
  • Protect passenger information
  • Follow Pool Up community standards
