How It Works

A Simple Way to Organise Shared Work Travel

Pool Up is not an instant taxi service.

We organise regular work routes by matching people travelling from similar areas, at similar times, to similar employment destinations.

For Commuters

Register

Provide your general pickup area, workplace area, shift starting time and required travel days.

You do not need to provide your complete home address during the first registration.

Get Matched

Our team reviews the registered demand and searches for a suitable verified driver or approved transport partner.

Review the Trip

You receive the proposed meeting point, departure time, price, driver information and route conditions.

Add Credit

Prepaid ride credit is added after your payment has been confirmed.

Confirm Your Seat

The required credit is allocated to the booking and your seat is confirmed.

Complete the Journey

After the trip, you can confirm completion, rate your experience or report a concern.

For Drivers

Apply

Tell us about yourself, your vehicle, your regular work route and your available seats.

Complete Verification

Your identity, driving licence, vehicle documents and relevant compliance information are reviewed.

Publish Your Route

Approved drivers provide their regular route, available days, departure time and number of available seats.

Accept Suitable Commuters

You receive information about the proposed passenger and meeting arrangement before accepting.

Complete the Journey

Follow the confirmed route, meeting point and community standards.

Receive Your Contribution

Your driver amount is recorded after the journey is completed, subject to the agreed payout process and service fee.

How Pool Up V1 Works

The first version of Pool Up is supported by our team through the website, online forms and WhatsApp.

Route matching, verification, credit confirmation and booking support may be completed manually while we learn which work corridors have the strongest demand.

The public website explains the service and collects registrations.

A future member app will make matching, booking, ride credit, trip records, ratings and support faster.

The technology will change. The purpose will remain the same.
