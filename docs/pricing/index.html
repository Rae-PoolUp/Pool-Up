Pricing And Ride Credit

Clear Pricing. No Awkward Cash Exchanges.

Pool Up uses prepaid ride credit for confirmed journeys.

Passengers see the full trip price before deciding whether to book.

Drivers see the amount they will receive before accepting the journey.

How Ride Credit Works

Add Credit

Pay through an approved Pool Up payment method.

Payment Confirmation

Your Pool Up credit balance is updated once the payment has been confirmed.

Book a Journey

The required credit is allocated when your seat is confirmed.

Complete the Trip

After the journey is completed, the driver or transport partner amount is processed according to the booking terms.

Keep Unused Credit

Unused standard ride credit remains available for a future booking or may be handled according to the refund policy.

Sponsored credits may have separate conditions and may not be exchanged for cash.

Our Service Fee

Pool Up earns a small and transparent service fee from completed bookings.

This helps cover:

  • Member verification
  • Route coordination
  • Payment administration
  • Customer support
  • Driver support
  • Platform development
  • Community route growth

There should be no hidden deductions.

The passenger sees the full amount before booking.

The driver sees the expected driver amount before accepting.

No Cash Inside the Vehicle

Passengers should not pay a driver directly unless Pool Up has provided written instructions for an approved exception.

Drivers may not add charges after a trip has been confirmed.

Cancellations and Refunds

Cancellation conditions are shown before a booking is confirmed.

Refund eligibility may depend on:

  • Who cancelled
  • How early the cancellation was made
  • Whether the driver had already started the journey
  • Whether the credit was purchased or sponsored
  • Any non-refundable third-party costs
