Safety And Verification

Trust Is Not a Slogan. It Is a Process.

Pool Up is built around verified membership, clear trip records and responsible behaviour.

No transport service can remove every possible risk, but proper checks and clear procedures can reduce avoidable risks.

Driver Checks

Drivers must complete the required identity, licence, vehicle and compliance checks before receiving confirmed passenger bookings.

Additional checks may apply to certain routes or transport categories.

Commuter Checks

Passengers may be required to verify their identity, mobile number, work route and emergency contact information.

False information may result in removal from the platform.

Safer Meeting Arrangements

Initial pickup points should be:

  • Easy to identify
  • Safe for stopping
  • Well lit when possible
  • Away from dangerous intersections
  • Suitable for both the passenger and driver

Exact home addresses should not be published publicly.

Trip Records

Pool Up records the confirmed driver, passenger, route, meeting time and booking details.

Information is handled according to the Pool Up privacy policy and applicable data protection requirements.

Community Standards

Pool Up has zero tolerance for:

  • Harassment
  • Threats
  • Discrimination
  • Dangerous driving
  • Driving under the influence
  • Weapons inside vehicles
  • Unauthorised passengers
  • Cash demands
  • Deliberate route changes
  • Abuse of personal information

Reporting a Concern

Safety concerns should be reported as soon as possible.

In an immediate emergency, contact emergency services first.

Pool Up should then be informed once it is safe to do so.
