Accessibility And Inclusion

Work Travel Should Include Everyone

People with disabilities should have the same opportunity to reach work, training and community programmes.

Pool Up aims to create a respectful registration and matching process where passengers can explain the support they require.

Tell Us What Support Is Needed

Passengers may provide information about:

  • Mobility devices
  • Vehicle space requirements
  • Boarding assistance
  • A required companion
  • Communication requirements
  • Service animals
  • Safe meeting points
  • Any other relevant travel support

A diagnosis is not the same as a transport requirement.

We focus on what is needed for the journey to be completed safely and respectfully.

Confirmation Before Booking

Accessibility varies between vehicles and routes.

Pool Up will not confirm an accessible booking until the vehicle, driver and required support have been checked.

Where Pool Up cannot provide a suitable option, we will communicate this honestly.

Partner-Supported Mobility

Foundations, employers and sponsors can fund accessible ride credit or support the cost of a suitable specialist transport provider.
