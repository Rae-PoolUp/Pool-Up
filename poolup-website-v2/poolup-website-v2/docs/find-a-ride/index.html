What Happens After Registration?

We Review Your Route

Your route is grouped with other commuters travelling in a similar direction.

We Contact You

When a possible match becomes available, our team contacts you to confirm your information.

You Complete Verification

Basic identity and contact verification must be completed before a booking can be confirmed.

You Review the Offer

You receive the route, meeting point, departure time, driver details and full price.

There is no obligation to accept a proposed trip.

No Route Available Yet?

Your registration remains part of the route demand list.

We will contact you when enough demand and a suitable driver or transport partner become available.

We do not promise a route before it is ready.
