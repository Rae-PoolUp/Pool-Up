Community Impact

A Shared Ride Can Protect More Than a Morning

Reliable transport can help protect a shift, an income, an interview, a training opportunity and a household budget.

Pool Up is designed to create value for the passenger, driver and wider community.

More Affordable Work Travel

Sharing a route can reduce the cost pressure placed on individual commuters.

The final saving depends on the route, number of passengers and transport arrangement.

Better Use of Available Seats

Drivers can receive a contribution towards a journey they already make.

This helps keep value within local households.

Access to Opportunity

A person cannot benefit from a job, course or programme they cannot physically reach.

Pool Up helps organisations connect transport support to employment support.

Community Ride Fund

Pool Up is developing a Community Ride Fund to support approved commuters who cannot fully afford their work journey.

The fund may be supported through:

  • Foundation sponsorships
  • Employer contributions
  • Corporate social investment
  • Optional passenger contributions
  • Community fundraising
  • A portion of Pool Up service fees when financially sustainable

Sponsored credit is allocated to approved journeys and is not paid out as cash.

Measuring What Matters

As Pool Up grows, we intend to report real results such as:

  • Active work routes
  • Completed journeys
  • Commuters supported
  • Driver contributions paid
  • Sponsored ride credit used
  • Workplaces connected
  • Average passenger savings where measurable

We will publish actual results rather than untested claims.
