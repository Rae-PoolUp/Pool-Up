About Us

A Transport Idea Born During a Difficult Time

Pool Up was established during the COVID-19 period.

The pandemic showed how quickly a transport problem could become an employment problem.

When people could not reach work, interviews, training or essential services, opportunities disappeared.

It also showed the strength of communities.

People shared information, food, transport and support because they understood that progress depends on people helping one another.

During this period, we saw vehicles travelling with open seats while workers along the same routes struggled with expensive or unreliable transport.

Pool Up was created to close that gap.

We are building a community work travel network where verified people travelling in the same direction can share the journey and share the cost.

Our Mission

To make daily work travel more affordable, reliable and accessible by connecting verified commuters, drivers and transport partners travelling along similar routes.

Our Vision

A South Africa where transport cost and distance do not decide who gets to work, keeps a job or reaches an opportunity.

Our Values

People First

Every route begins with a real person who needs to reach work safely and with dignity.

Opportunity Within Reach

Transport should connect people to employment, education, training and progress.

Open and Fair

Prices, service fees, driver contributions and booking conditions must be clearly explained.

Local Progress

We build routes around the needs of local workers, businesses and communities.
