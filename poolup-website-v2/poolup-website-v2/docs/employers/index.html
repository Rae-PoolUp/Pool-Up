Employers

Help Your People Arrive Ready for Work

Transport challenges can affect punctuality, attendance, staff wellbeing and employee retention.

Pool Up helps employers understand where staff travel from and whether shared work routes can be created.

Employer Route Survey

We collect general staff travel information such as:

  • Pickup suburb
  • Workplace location
  • Shift starting time
  • Travel days
  • Current transport challenges
  • Accessibility requirements

The information helps identify groups of employees travelling along similar routes.

Employer-Supported Ride Credit

Employers can contribute towards staff transport through allocated Pool Up ride credit.

Credit can be limited by employee, route, date or approved journey type.

Shift-Based Transport Pilots

Pool Up can help organise a pilot route for selected shifts, departments or workplace locations.

The pilot may use verified carpool drivers, an approved transport partner or a combination of both.

Why Work With Pool Up?

  • Support more reliable work attendance
  • Help staff manage transport costs
  • Understand staff travel demand
  • Create routes around real shift times
  • Support local drivers and transport operators
  • Demonstrate measurable community impact
